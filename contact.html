FREQUENTLY ASKED

Can't find the answer? Call us or drop a message using the form above.

Do you offer delivery across Bihar? +
Yes — we deliver across Patna same-day for orders placed before 12 PM. For other districts in Bihar, delivery typically takes 1–3 business days depending on location. Bulk orders get free delivery.
What is the minimum order quantity? +
There's no minimum order for retail purchases — you can buy a single sheet. For wholesale and trade pricing, a minimum of 20 sheets applies. Contact us for bulk pricing quotes.
Can you do custom sizes / cutting? +
Absolutely. We have precision cutting equipment in-house. You can specify custom dimensions for plywood, MDF, and block board when placing your order. Additional cutting charges may apply.
How do I earn and use loyalty points? +
Every product you buy has a unique QR code label. Scan it after purchase using your Xtraaplywood account to earn points. Points accumulate in your wallet and can be redeemed for discounts, free products, and other rewards once you hit the required threshold.
Are all products ISI certified? +
Most of our plywood and structural products are ISI certified (BIS-licensed) and comply with IS:710 (BWP), IS:303 (BWR), and IS:2191 standards. Each product listing indicates its certification grade clearly.
Do you supply to contractors and builders? +
Yes — we work with carpenters, interior designers, contractors, builders, and architects across Bihar. Trade accounts get priority pricing, dedicated support, and credit terms. Contact us to open a trade account.
