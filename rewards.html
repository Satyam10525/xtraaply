HOW IT WORKS

  • 01
    Buy any Xtraaplywood product

    Every product we sell comes with a unique QR code label attached to it.

  • 02
    Scan or enter the code

    Use the camera scanner above or type in the alphanumeric code printed below the QR.

  • 03
    Points credited instantly

    Points appear in your wallet right away. Each code can only be scanned once — ever.

  • 04
    Reach your threshold

    Accumulate enough points to unlock your tier level and access bigger rewards.

  • 05
    Redeem for discounts & rewards

    Use points for cash discounts on your next purchase, free products, or exclusive deals.
